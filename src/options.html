    Privacy

    Everything in your glossary and your settings stays on this device. The extension sends text to an outside service only when you explicitly ask for an explanation and the offline glossary doesn't already have it — and then it sends only that single word or phrase to the dictionary or Wikipedia, nothing else. There are no accounts, no tracking, no analytics, and no API keys. Turn off both online sources above to run fully offline.
